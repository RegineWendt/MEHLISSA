# Draft Feasibility Request - Lassen 2023 Pulmonary Transit Cohort

## Status and deliberate limitation

This is a feasibility-only draft, not a participant-data request, a record of
external contact, or granted access. PCQ-C2 remains blocked because the study's
pulmonary-trunk-to-left-atrium bolus transit time is not the same observable as
MEHLISSA's current capillary-only residence time. The project must establish
and independently review an observation model for the additional pre-capillary,
post-capillary, and mixing delays without fitting it to Lassen outcomes before
participant records may be requested or inspected.

## Suggested recipient

Dr Martin Lyngby Lassen. The article lists
`martin.lyngby.lassen@regionh.dk` for reprint requests. Verify that address on
an institutional or publisher page immediately before sending.

## Suggested subject

Feasibility inquiry about de-identified pulmonary transit records from Lassen
et al. 2023

## Suggested message

Dear Dr Lassen,

We are developing MEHLISSA, an open research simulator for multiscale medical
transport and physiological scenarios. We are assessing whether an independent
whole-pulmonary observation model could support a future, prospectively locked
comparison with the measurements reported in your 2023 article, "Pulmonary
blood volume assessment from a standard cardiac rubidium-82 imaging protocol:
impact of adenosine-induced hyperemia."

At this stage, we are **not asking you to send participant-level records**. We
would first like to establish whether a later non-commercial academic data
request would be feasible and what methodological and governance conditions
would apply.

Could you please let us know whether pseudonymous participant-level values are
still retained for the following items at rest, during adenosine stress, and
for the available test-retest sessions:

- pulmonary-trunk-to-left-atrium mean bolus transit time;
- heart rate and stroke volume;
- cardiac output, with a clear flag for measured versus derived values;
- the reported pulmonary blood volume, clearly marked as derived;
- body surface area, age in whole years or an approved age band, and sex;
- replicate identity, acquisition order, missingness, quality flags, and
  method-specific uncertainty; and
- the measurement metadata or data dictionary needed to interpret the arrival
  threshold, time-activity processing, reconstruction, motion handling, and
  anatomical boundaries.

We understand that pulmonary blood volume in the article is defined from flow
and transit time. We would therefore not use it as independent evidence for a
volume-flow-transit closure test. We also understand that the reported transit
includes vascular and mixing components outside the capillary-only quantity in
our current model and may be affected by tracer retention. Those limitations
will remain explicit and no model term or tolerance will be fitted to your
participant outcomes.

Before considering a later formal data request, could you also advise:

- whether sharing such a minimal de-identified extract would be compatible
  with the original consent and ethics approval;
- whether the cohort or source scans overlap another publication that should
  be treated as the same evidence family;
- whether only derived tabular values or also processed time-activity curves
  could in principle be made available;
- the applicable processing, retention, security, publication, and
  redistribution terms; and
- the required data-use agreement, institutional signatory, citation, and
  acknowledgment.

If the route appears feasible, we would complete independent review of the
observation model and institutional data-governance arrangements before making
a formal request. Any later records would remain outside the public repository,
and only permitted non-identifying aggregate results would be published.

Thank you for considering this preliminary inquiry.
